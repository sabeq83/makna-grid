# COMPLIANCE_GUIDE

Version: 1.0

---

# 1. Purpose

This knowledge base guides Gemini AI in generating content that is safe, accurate, non-deceptive, and suitable for publication across social media and affiliate marketing platforms.

The guide is designed to reduce the risk of:

- Misleading claims
- Unsupported product promises
- Harmful health statements
- Manipulative promotion
- Platform policy violations
- Misrepresentation of products or results

---

# 2. Scope

The Compliance Guide applies to:

- Voice-over scripts
- Captions
- Titles
- Descriptions
- Hooks
- Calls to action
- Product mentions
- Affiliate promotion
- Educational claims
- On-screen text

The Compliance Guide does not replace:

- Local laws
- Platform-specific policies
- Advertising regulations
- Product labeling requirements
- Legal review
- Medical review

When a platform rule, law, or verified product document is stricter than this guide, follow the stricter rule.

---

# 3. Core Compliance Principles

## 3.1 Accuracy

Only state information supported by:

- The content brief
- Verified product information
- Approved product documentation
- Reliable source material
- Clearly observable visual evidence

Do not invent:

- Product features
- Product ingredients
- Certifications
- Test results
- Discounts
- Stock conditions
- Customer results
- Scientific evidence

## 3.2 Transparency

Make the commercial relationship clear when required.

Do not disguise:

- Affiliate promotion
- Sponsored content
- Paid partnerships
- Product recommendations
- Promotional intent

## 3.3 Non-Deception

Do not create a false or misleading impression through:

- Wording
- Editing
- Visuals
- Before-and-after comparisons
- Selective information
- Exaggerated demonstrations
- Artificial urgency

## 3.4 Proportionality

The strength of the claim must match the strength of the evidence.

General product information may use neutral wording.

Strong claims require strong and verified evidence.

## 3.5 Audience Safety

Avoid content that may encourage:

- Unsafe product use
- Dangerous experiments
- Self-diagnosis
- Delayed professional treatment
- Excessive consumption
- Misuse of tools or ingredients

# 4. Claim Classification

## 4.1 Observable Claim

A claim based on something directly visible in the content.

Examples:

- The container has a locking lid.
- The mixture becomes smoother after blending.
- The product is used to store dry ingredients.
- The tool helps cut the ingredient into smaller pieces.

Observable claims are allowed when the visual clearly supports them.

## 4.2 Factual Product Claim

A claim about a product feature or specification.

Examples:

- Capacity
- Material
- Dimensions
- Included accessories
- Product variant
- Usage instructions

Use only when verified by approved product information.

## 4.3 Performance Claim

A claim about what a product can do.

Examples:

- Saves preparation time
- Helps organize ingredients
- Reduces manual effort
- Keeps items separated

Use cautious wording unless supported by reliable evidence.

Preferred wording:

- Designed to help
- Can make the process easier
- May reduce preparation steps
- Useful for
- Suitable for
- Helps support

Avoid absolute wording unless fully verified.

## 4.4 Health Claim

A claim related to:

- Disease
- Symptoms
- Treatment
- Prevention
- Recovery
- Body function
- Mental health
- Weight loss
- Medical outcomes

Health claims require the highest level of caution.

Do not make direct medical claims unless the claim is explicitly approved and supported by authoritative documentation.

## 4.5 Comparative Claim

A claim that compares one product, method, or result with another.

Examples:

- Better
- Faster
- Safer
- Healthier
- More effective
- More durable
- Number one
- Best

Use only when the comparison is specific, fair, and supported.

Avoid vague superiority claims.

# 5. Health and Wellness Rules

## 5.1 General Rule

Do not present food, beverages, herbs, supplements, oils, tools, or household products as treatments for medical conditions.

## 5.2 Prohibited Health Claims

Do not claim that a product or ingredient:

- Cures a disease
- Treats a disease
- Prevents a disease
- Replaces medicine
- Replaces medical care
- Guarantees recovery
- Eliminates symptoms
- Produces guaranteed weight loss
- Permanently changes body function
- Works for everyone

Avoid statements such as:

- Cures diabetes
- Eliminates high blood pressure
- Heals joint pain
- Treats hemorrhoids
- Removes toxins
- Burns fat instantly
- Guarantees better sleep
- Replaces prescribed medicine
- Safe for all medical conditions

## 5.3 Safer Health Wording

When supported by reliable source information, use neutral wording such as:

- Traditionally used in
- Commonly consumed as
- Contains
- Provides
- Can be included as part of
- May support
- Often used for
- Suitable for general wellness routines

Do not use safer wording to disguise an unverified medical claim.

## 5.4 Ingredient Claims

Ingredient-level information must remain:

- General
- Non-diagnostic
- Non-curative
- Supported by reliable information
- Relevant to ordinary consumption or use

Do not transfer an ingredient claim directly to a finished product unless supported.

## 5.5 Medical Disclaimer

A disclaimer does not make an unsupported claim acceptable.

Do not write a prohibited medical claim and then add:

- Results may vary.
- Consult a doctor.
- This is not medical advice.

The claim itself must already be compliant.

---

# 6. Food and Beverage Rules

Do not:

- Claim that a recipe guarantees a medical result.
- Label a recipe as healthy without reasonable context.
- Present excessive consumption as beneficial.
- Hide important preparation risks.
- Recommend unsafe storage practices.
- State unsupported shelf-life durations.
- Present raw or undercooked food as universally safe.

When relevant, include practical safety information such as:

- Proper storage
- Refrigeration
- Cooking temperature
- Clean preparation
- Allergy awareness
- Portion context

Avoid absolute terms such as:

- Completely healthy
- Zero risk
- Safe for everyone
- Detoxes the body
- Guilt-free
- Unlimited consumption

---

# 7. Affiliate Marketing Rules

Affiliate content must:

- Accurately represent the product.
- Show a relevant use case.
- Avoid false scarcity.
- Avoid fabricated discounts.
- Avoid guaranteed outcomes.
- Avoid misleading product demonstrations.
- Avoid implying personal ownership or use when not true.
- Avoid presenting assumptions as verified facts.

## 7.1 Affiliate Disclosure

When disclosure is required, use clear wording appropriate to the platform.

Examples:

- Affiliate link
- I may earn a commission from qualifying purchases.
- This post contains an affiliate recommendation.
- Product link may generate a commission.

The disclosure must be:

- Easy to notice
- Easy to understand
- Close to the promotional content
- Written in the output language

Do not hide disclosure inside unrelated hashtags or long blocks of text.

## 7.2 Product Availability

Do not claim:

- Limited stock
- Final units
- Ending today
- Almost sold out
- Flash sale
- Lowest price

Unless the information is current and verified.

Preferred wording:

- Check the current price.
- See current availability.
- View the latest product details.
- Check the current offer in the product listing.

## 7.3 Pricing

Do not invent or assume:

- Product price
- Discount percentage
- Voucher value
- Shipping cost
- Cashback
- Promotional period

Use current verified data or neutral wording.

---

# 8. Before-and-After Rules

Before-and-after content must not:

- Misrepresent results
- Use unrelated footage
- Change lighting to exaggerate improvement
- Change angles to create a false comparison
- Present exceptional results as typical
- Hide important conditions
- Claim guaranteed outcomes

If the result is a demonstration, describe it as a demonstration.

If the result may vary, do not imply universality.

Do not use before-and-after framing for sensitive medical, body, or financial claims without verified compliance approval.

---

# 9. Testimonial and Social Proof Rules

Do not invent:

- Customer reviews
- Expert endorsements
- Sales figures
- Ratings
- User counts
- Awards
- Certifications
- Media mentions

Testimonials must be:

- Real
- Authorized
- Accurately represented
- Relevant to the claim
- Free from unsupported medical or financial promises

Do not rewrite a testimonial in a way that strengthens the original claim.

Do not present one person's experience as a guaranteed result.

---

# 10. Expert and Authority Rules

Do not imply endorsement from:

- Doctors
- Nutritionists
- Government agencies
- Universities
- Laboratories
- Religious authorities
- Professional organizations

Unless the endorsement is real, current, authorized, and verifiable.

Do not use:

- Doctor-approved
- Clinically proven
- Scientifically guaranteed
- Expert recommended
- Government certified

Without verified evidence.

---

# 11. Certification and Legal Status Rules

Only mention certifications, registrations, or approvals when verified.

Examples include:

- Product registration
- Halal certification
- Food safety certification
- Laboratory testing
- Organic certification
- Quality standards

Do not assume that:

- A seller claim is verified.
- One product variant shares another variant's certification.
- A brand-level certificate applies to every product.
- Registration means medical effectiveness.

Preserve exact certification wording.

Do not strengthen or reinterpret it.

---

# 12. Financial and Income Claim Rules

Do not claim or imply:

- Guaranteed income
- Guaranteed profit
- Risk-free earnings
- Instant financial success
- Fixed conversion results
- Predictable sales outcomes
- Universal business performance

Avoid statements such as:

- Earn money automatically.
- Guaranteed to make millions.
- Anyone can succeed instantly.
- Zero risk.
- This strategy always works.

Preferred wording:

- May create an income opportunity.
- Results depend on execution and market conditions.
- Can be used as part of an affiliate strategy.
- Performance varies by account, audience, product, and platform.

Do not fabricate revenue screenshots, sales data, or testimonials.

---

# 13. Safety Rules for Product Use

Do not provide instructions that encourage:

- Dangerous tool use
- Unsafe electrical use
- Improper chemical mixing
- Excessive heat exposure
- Unsafe food storage
- Use beyond product instructions
- Use by unsuitable age groups
- Use on damaged skin
- Ingestion of non-food products

When the content includes a potentially hazardous tool or process:

- Follow manufacturer instructions.
- Include necessary precautions.
- Avoid presenting unsafe shortcuts.
- Do not remove protective equipment for visual appeal.

---

# 14. Sensitive Audience Rules

Use additional caution when content may reach:

- Children
- Pregnant people
- Older adults
- People with medical conditions
- People taking medication
- Financially vulnerable audiences

Do not make personalized recommendations for sensitive groups without verified expert guidance.

Use neutral wording such as:

- Check whether this is suitable for your needs.
- Follow the product instructions.
- Consult a qualified professional when necessary.

---

# 15. Prohibited Promotional Patterns

Never use:

- False urgency
- Fake scarcity
- Guaranteed outcomes
- Fear-based selling
- Shame-based selling
- Medical misinformation
- Fabricated authority
- Fabricated testimonials
- Hidden affiliate intent
- Misleading editing
- Unsupported superlatives
- Unverified comparison claims
- Manipulative engagement bait

Avoid patterns such as:

- Buy this before your health gets worse.
- Everyone with this problem needs this product.
- Doctors do not want you to know this.
- This works instantly.
- Guaranteed results.
- The number one product.
- The safest product available.
- Only intelligent people use this.
- You will regret not buying this.
- This product changes your life.

# 16. Preferred Language Rules

Prefer:

- Can help
- Designed to
- Useful for
- Suitable for
- May support
- Commonly used for
- Can make the process easier
- Based on the product information
- According to the verified product details
- Results may differ depending on use

Use these phrases only when truthful and contextually appropriate.

Avoid:

- Guaranteed
- Instantly
- Permanently
- Completely safe
- Works for everyone
- No side effects
- Risk-free
- Miracle
- Secret cure
- Proven best
- Number one

# 17. Decision Rules

Before generating any claim:

1. Identify the claim type.
2. Check whether the claim is supported.
3. Check whether the wording is stronger than the evidence.
4. Check whether the claim involves health, money, safety, or legal status.
5. Replace absolute wording with accurate wording when appropriate.
6. Remove the claim if reliable support is unavailable.
7. Confirm that the CTA does not create false urgency.
8. Confirm that the visual direction does not misrepresent the result.
9. Add disclosure when required.
10. Preserve exact verified product information.

If verification is unavailable:

- Do not guess.
- Do not invent.
- Use neutral wording.
- Request verified information in systems that allow clarification.
- Omit the unsupported detail when clarification is not possible.

---

# 18. Compliance Priority Rules

Apply rules in the following order:

1. Applicable law
2. Platform policy
3. Verified product documentation
4. Compliance Guide
5. Brand Voice
6. Copywriting preference
7. Creative style

Creative goals must never override compliance.

---

# 19. Compliance Review Checklist

Before finalizing content, verify:

- Are all factual claims supported?
- Are health claims neutral and non-curative?
- Are financial claims non-guaranteed?
- Is the affiliate relationship disclosed when required?
- Is pricing current and verified?
- Is urgency real and verified?
- Are testimonials authentic?
- Are certifications accurately stated?
- Is the product shown honestly?
- Are instructions safe?
- Is the CTA non-coercive?
- Does the content avoid unsupported superiority claims?
- Does the content avoid manipulative engagement?
- Does the output match the actual product and video?

If any answer is uncertain, revise or remove the affected statement.

---

# 20. Output Specification

Every compliance review must include:

- `compliance_status`
- `risk_level`
- `flagged_claims`
- `required_revisions`
- `approved_copy`

Allowed `compliance_status` values:

- `approved`
- `approved_with_revision`
- `rejected`

Allowed `risk_level` values:

- `low`
- `medium`
- `high`

Example:

```json
{
  "compliance_status": "approved_with_revision",
  "risk_level": "medium",
  "flagged_claims": [
    {
      "original_text": "This drink cures high blood pressure.",
      "issue": "Unsupported medical treatment claim."
    }
  ],
  "required_revisions": [
    "Remove the disease treatment claim.",
    "Replace it with neutral ingredient or usage information supported by verified sources."
  ],
  "approved_copy": "This drink combines commonly used ingredients for a simple daily beverage."
}
```

Every approved output must be:

- Accurate
- Non-deceptive
- Non-coercive
- Supported by available information
- Safe for the intended audience
- Consistent with platform requirements
- Ready for publication
